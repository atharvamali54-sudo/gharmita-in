Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Cancellation by Customer

Customers can cancel a booked appointment free of charge before the service provider has been dispatched or has arrived at the location. If a cancellation is requested after the service partner is already on the way or has arrived, a nominal inspection / travel fee may apply.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are applicable in the following scenarios:

  • Customer cancelled the booking within the permitted free cancellation window.
  • The service partner was unable to fulfill the service or did not show up.
  • Duplicate payments or excess deductions occurred during online checkout.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund is approved, the amount will be credited back to the original payment source (bank account, UPI, credit/debit card) within 5 to 7 business days, as per standard banking procedures.

4. Worker Wallet Balance Policy

Funds added to worker wallets are used for platform service commissions and security deposits. In case of account deactivation or closure, remaining wallet balances can be refunded to the worker's verified bank account upon request after clearing pending settlement dues.
